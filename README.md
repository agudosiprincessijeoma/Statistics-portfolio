Five end-to-end projects using public data. Each one is written up the way I would present it to a client or a research team: a clear question, a description of the data, the method and why I chose it, the analysis, the results, what they mean, and an honest account of the limitations. All code is included, so every result can be reproduced.

| # | Project | Tool | Methods | Data |

## Highlights

* **Project 1:** Nigeria ranked 19th of 52 African countries for income in 2007 but only 40th for life expectancy, which points to factors beyond wealth.
* **Project 2:** students who want to go on to higher education scored almost two grade points higher and were twice as likely to pass.
* **Project 3:** a smoker x obesity interaction lifted explained variance from 76% to 87% and cut prediction error by almost 40%.
* **Project 4:** smoking in pregnancy was linked to babies 284 g lighter and roughly doubled the odds of low birth weight after adjusting for other risk factors.
* **Project 5:** discounts above 20% wiped out US$135,000 of profit, almost half the company's total.
